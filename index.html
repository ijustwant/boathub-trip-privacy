Privacy Policy – BoatHub Trip

Last updated: September 18, 2026

BoatHub Trip is a simple GPS trip logger for Android. This page explains what data the app uses and how it is handled.

What data does the app collect?

BoatHub Trip uses your phone's GPS position (precise location) to record your route while a trip is in progress, including in the background when the app isn't open on screen. This is necessary so the app can log your entire trip, even while the screen is off or you're using other apps.

How is the data used?

Location data is stored exclusively on your own device, as GPX files (a standard GPS track format). These files are saved in your phone's own storage and are available to you to view, export, or delete as you choose.

Is data shared with anyone?

No. BoatHub Trip does not send location data, GPX files, or any other information to any external server, third party, or ad network. The app has no network connection for sharing data outside the device. Everything stays on your phone.

Storage and deletion

GPX files remain on the device until you delete them yourself, either through the app or your phone's file manager. Uninstalling the app may also remove the app's own data, depending on your phone's operating system settings.

Permissions the app requests

  • Precise location – to record the GPS track during a trip.
  • Background location – so tracking continues even when the app isn't shown on screen.
  • Notifications – to show an ongoing notification while a trip is being logged (required by Android for background GPS services).

Changes to this policy

If this privacy policy changes, the date at the top of this page will be updated. Significant changes will be made clear in the app's own store listing.

Contact

Questions about privacy in BoatHub Trip can be directed to the developer via the contact information listed in the app's Play Store listing.
